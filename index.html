Useful Commands

/geyser offhand

Quick-swap to offhand

Puts whatever's in your main hand into your offhand instead. Great for luring cows and other animals with wheat while keeping a tool ready in your main hand.

/geyser advancements

Advancement tracker

Opens an advancements tab just like the full Java game, with a full list of fun challenges to chase.

/geyser tooltips

Exact durability

Adds tooltips showing the precise durability left on your tools — no more guessing when your pickaxe is about to break.

/skins

Skin menu

Opens a menu to apply a skin everyone else on the server can see. You can also run /skin (username) to wear your favorite YouTuber's skin.

/tell

Private messages

Lets you privately message one other player — works in normal Education Edition too, not just here.
